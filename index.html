1. Introduction

NestHouse is a flexible, multi-industry Online Store 2.0 theme. It ships with four theme styles — NestHouse, Selvedge, Camellia and Graphite — and a token-driven design system, so you can restyle the whole store from the theme editor without touching code.

Highlights: sections on every template, drag-and-drop blocks, native color schemes, predictive search, faceted filtering, quick view, product recommendations, selling plans, Shop Pay Installments, pickup availability, a light/dark switch for shoppers, storefront translations in 11 languages, and full accessibility support.

2. Installation

  • 1. Download the theme package (.zip) from your Theme Store purchase.
  • 2. In Shopify admin go to Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload zip file.
  • 3. Select the downloaded .zip and upload.
  • 4. Click Edit theme to open the theme editor.
  • 5. (Optional) Make it your live theme when you are ready: click Publish on the theme card.

Tip: Always customize on an unpublished/draft copy first, then publish when ready.

3. Choosing a theme style

NestHouse ships with four theme styles. Each one changes colors, corner radius, and section spacing — the same store, a different look.

Theme styleBest forLook
NestHouseFurniture, homewareWarm cream and walnut, generous spacing, story-led
SelvedgeClothing, accessoriesCrisp white, sharp corners, product-forward
CamelliaBeauty, wellnessSoft blush tones, rounded corners, tactile
GraphiteElectronics, hardwareDark surfaces, high contrast, compact

To switch:

  • 1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online store.
  • 2. Find the theme that you want to edit, and then click Edit theme.
  • 3. Click the Theme settings icon.
  • 4. Click Theme style.
  • 5. Select the style you want, then click Change style.
  • 6. Click Save.

Switching a theme style changes the look only — your products, pages, menus, and section content stay exactly as they are.

Note: switching overwrites presentational settings such as colors and corner radius. If you have hand-picked your own colors, note them down first — they will be replaced.

4. Theme settings

Open the theme editor → Theme settings:

  • Logo — upload your logo and favicon; set the desktop logo width.
  • Color schemes — up to four reusable schemes (background, surface, text, buttons, borders). Each section can be assigned a scheme, so you can alternate light and dark bands down a page without touching code.
  • Colors — the 25 base colors used across the theme: backgrounds, surfaces, four levels of text, borders, primary and accent colors, the header background, and the dark footer & newsletter band (used when the footer Background tone is Dark).
  • Alternate color mode — the colors used when a visitor flips the Light/Dark switch in the announcement bar. Base mode says which look your store opens in: NestHouse, Selvedge and Camellia open light and the switch turns on a dark palette; Graphite opens dark and the switch turns on a light palette. Each theme style ships its own alternate palette, and every color here can be changed. Leave Solid button, Rating stars or Sale price text empty to keep the base colors. To hide the switch, turn off Show light/dark theme toggle in the announcement bar section.
  • Typography — choose a display font (headings) and a body font; adjust the heading scale ratio and body text size.
  • Layout — container width, padding, corner radius (card/button/input), section rhythm, and Product cards: Product image fit (Fit shows the whole product on a plain background — best for furniture and cut-out shots; Fill crops the photo to fill the card), Card background (Same as page or Surface color) and Card border strength.
  • Motion — card hover lift; respect the visitor’s “Reduce motion” OS setting.
  • Social media — Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok URLs.

Changes preview instantly in the editor. Click Save to keep them.

5. Header & navigation

  • Menus: set up navigation in Admin → Navigation, then pick your menu in the header section. Multi-level (mega/dropdown) menus are supported.
  • Search: predictive search suggests products, collections, pages, and articles as the shopper types.
  • Cart: the cart icon shows a live item count and opens the cart drawer.
  • Localization: country/currency and language selectors appear when your store has Markets and additional languages enabled (Admin → Settings → Markets / Languages).

6. Sections

Build any page from the theme editor with Add section. Nineteen sections are available on every template:

Hero and imagery — Hero · Hero slideshow · Lookbook · Lifestyle gallery · Category tiles Products — Product grid · Collection grid · Product recommendations · Complementary products · Recently viewed Story and trust — Brand story · Manifesto · Testimonial · Featured in · Trust badges · Trust and press strip Content — Blog posts · Newsletter · Contact form

Every section can be reordered, hidden, or removed, and most accept drag-and-drop blocks. Ten sections also accept app blocks, so apps you install can add content inside them: Brand story · Complementary products · Footer · Hero · Lookbook · Cart · Product · Manifesto · Newsletter · Testimonial.

A Custom Liquid section is available for anything the built-in sections do not cover.

7. Product pages

The product template is block-based — reorder or remove any block in the editor. Nine block types are available: Title, Price, Vendor, Rating, Description, Feature checklist, Specification row, Custom Liquid, and app blocks from apps you have installed.

Features:

  • Image gallery with zoom; video and 3D (model-viewer) media.
  • Variant selection with native swatches (color/image).
  • Quantity selector, Add to cart, and Buy it now (dynamic/accelerated checkout).
  • Pickup availability, Shop Pay Installments, Follow on Shop, back-in-stock notify.
  • Product recommendations (related) and complementary products.
  • Structured data (JSON-LD) for rich results.

Star ratings

The Rating block and the stars on product cards read Shopify's standard reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count metafields. Install any review app that writes to those metafields and your ratings appear automatically — on product cards, the product page, and in search results. No setup or code is needed.

Because the theme draws these stars itself, they always match your typography and colors. To change their color, go to Theme settings → Colors → Accent brass.

Products without ratings simply show no stars; nothing else changes.

To show the full review list and the review submission form, add your review app's block to the product page: Edit theme → Product page → Add block → App blocks.

8. Collections, search & filtering

  • Grid/list view, sorting, and pagination.
  • Faceted filtering by availability, price, and product attributes (enable filters in Admin → Online Store → Navigation → Filters, or via Search & Discovery app).
  • Quick view opens a product preview without leaving the page.
  • Unit pricing shows per-unit prices where required.

9. Cart & checkout

  • Cart drawer and full cart page.
  • Update quantity, remove items, add order notes.
  • Line-item properties, unit price, and selling plans (subscriptions) display.
  • Accelerated checkout buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay) when enabled in Admin → Settings → Payments.
  • App blocks (@app) are supported in the cart for compatible apps.
  • Gift cards: create a gift card product in Admin → Products → Gift cards. The theme includes the gift card page your customers receive by email, with the code, balance, QR code and an Add to Apple Wallet button.

10. Languages

NestHouse includes storefront translations for 11 languages: English, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Portugal), Spanish and Swedish. Buttons, labels, cart and account pages, and messages switch automatically to the shopper's language.

  • 1. Add and publish a language in Admin → Settings → Languages.
  • 2. To change any built-in wording, go to Online Store → Themes, click … next to the theme, then Edit default theme content.
  • 3. Your own content (product descriptions, pages, section text) is translated separately — for example with Shopify's free Translate & Adapt app.

A language selector appears in the footer once more than one language is published.

11. FAQ

Q: How do I change the theme’s colors and fonts? A: Theme editor → Theme settings → Colors / Typography. Changes preview instantly.

Q: Where do I edit the menu? A: Admin → Navigation. Then select the menu in the header section settings.

Q: The country/currency selector doesn’t show. Why? A: It appears only when your store has more than one market/currency or language enabled (Admin → Settings → Markets / Languages).

Q: How do I enable collection filters? A: Admin → Online Store → Navigation → Filters (or the Search & Discovery app).

Q: How do I show accelerated checkout (Shop Pay/Apple Pay) buttons? A: Enable them in Admin → Settings → Payments and turn on “dynamic checkout button.”

Q: How do I show star ratings on my products? A: Install any review app that writes to Shopify's standard reviews.rating metafield — the stars then appear on product cards and product pages on their own. Change their color under Theme settings → Colors → Accent brass. See section 7.

Q: Do I need to know code to use NestHouse? A: No. Everything is configured from the theme editor. Optional Custom Liquid sections/blocks are available for advanced users.

Q: My store doesn't look like the demo after installing. Why? A: Layout, colors, fonts and section settings install exactly as in the demo of the theme style you chose. Demo images, products and text do not transfer — add your own images in the theme editor and the sections fill in. Until then, sections show a placeholder or a sample image.

Q: Will updating the theme erase my settings? A: No. When a new version is released, Shopify shows a notification on your theme in Online Store → Themes. Click it, then Add to draft themes. Everything you set in the theme editor — theme settings, sections, blocks, templates and app embeds — is copied to the new version. Edits made directly to the theme code may not carry over; Shopify tells you on the theme card if they could not be included. Review the draft, then publish it.

Q: How do I translate my store? A: The theme's own wording is already translated into 11 languages — see section 10. Publish a language in Admin → Settings → Languages.

Q: Can I edit the theme code? A: Yes, but duplicate your theme first (Online Store → Themes → … → Duplicate) and edit the copy, so you can always go back. Code edits are not covered by theme support (see section 12). For custom development, we recommend hiring a Shopify Partner (opens in a new tab).

12. Support policy

Included with your purchase:

  • Answers to questions about the theme's built-in features and settings.
  • Fixes for bugs in the theme, such as a broken layout, a dead link or a feature that does not work as described in this documentation. Critical bugs are fixed first.
  • Free theme updates, released through Shopify (see the FAQ on updating).

Not included:

  • Custom design or code changes, and problems caused by edits to the theme code.
  • Installing, configuring or fixing third-party apps.
  • Store setup tasks outside the theme, such as products, payments, shipping or domains.

For these, we recommend hiring a Shopify Partner (opens in a new tab).
